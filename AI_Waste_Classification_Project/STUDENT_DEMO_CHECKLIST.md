# Classroom Demo Checklist

## Before the demo
- [ ] Install Python 3.10/3.11 and project requirements.
- [ ] Download and prepare TrashNet.
- [ ] Train the model and confirm `models/waste_classifier.keras` exists.
- [ ] Open the Streamlit app.
- [ ] Keep 6–10 clear sample images ready (one item per image).
- [ ] Test camera permission and internet-independent app startup.

## Suggested 8-minute demonstration
1. Explain the waste-segregation problem (1 minute).
2. Show the six training categories and a few dataset images (1 minute).
3. Explain CNN and transfer learning with a simple diagram on the board (2 minutes).
4. Upload a sample plastic bottle image and show the prediction (1 minute).
5. Try a metal can, paper and cardboard image (1 minute).
6. Show training accuracy/loss and confusion matrix (1 minute).
7. Discuss one incorrect/uncertain prediction and future improvements (1 minute).

## Suggested student extension
Ask students to collect 5–10 additional photos per category on campus (only safe, clean objects), label them with teacher review, and discuss how additional data could improve real-world performance.
